- **How to use this atlas**
  - Each bold dotted number identifies exactly one item; for example, `1.3.2` is the runtime-partition decision. References such as `see 1.3.3` refer to these IDs, not line numbers or automatically numbered Markdown lists.
  - This is a historical, append-only decision record: add the next unused child number at the end of its parent's children. Never renumber, reorder, remove or reuse an existing ID. A changed choice gets a new ID that says which earlier ID it supersedes; the old choice remains visible.
  - A new child may be inserted in the file under its existing parent; append-only applies to each parent's logical child sequence, not the physical end of the Markdown file. Concurrent edits to one parent must reconcile ID allocation before merging.
  - Bracketed status describes evidence **on 2026-10-11**, not live completion tracking. `implemented` means demonstrated in current code/docs; `implemented in reference` is explicitly not a production distributed deployment; `agreed design; pending` comes from existing open design issues.
  - Source links point to the inspected repository commit or issue. Existing ADRs/specifications remain authoritative for their detailed contracts; this atlas provides durable IDs and cross-decision navigation, not duplicate implementation specs.

  - **1.1** Who is allowed to change gameplay

([agent contract](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/AGENTS.md); [architecture](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md))

([#146](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/146); [agent contract](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/AGENTS.md))
    - **1.1.3** [implemented] `mmorpg-core` owns zone gameplay, `physics-engine` owns physical outcomes, and `mmorpg-control-plane` owns placement/fencing metadata without mutating zone state. ([agent contract](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/AGENTS.md); [architecture](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md))
    - **1.1.4** [implemented] Reusable session, transport, reconnection, tick and recovery semantics belong to `game-server`, not a second MMORPG implementation. See 4.1.1. ([agent contract](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/AGENTS.md); [architecture](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md))

  - **1.3** Logical zones, partitions and overload
    - **1.3.1** [agreed design; pending] Logical-zone identity, content revision, persistence namespace, and authoritative timeline survive runtime repartitioning. ([#146](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/146))
    - **1.3.2** [agreed design; pending] Runtime partitions are spatially contiguous and may split, move, merge or migrate as load changes. ([#146](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/146))
    - **1.3.3** [agreed design; pending] Boundaries move around connected interaction islands; each island has one mutation owner on one machine. Cross-boundary ghost data may be read, never mutated by the neighbor. ([#146](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/146))
    - **1.3.4** [agreed design; pending] Partitions within one logical zone advance at a shared tick barrier, never on divergent simulation timelines. ([#146](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/146))
    - **1.3.5** [agreed design; pending] Overload preserves the fixed simulation timestep and slows the whole logical zone (time dilation), rather than increasing timestep length. ([#146](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/146))
    - **1.3.6** [agreed design; pending] Game mechanics use simulation time; auctions, schedules and offline obligations require explicitly supplied real-world time. See 1.5.3. ([#146](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/146); [#144](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/144))
  - **1.4** Cross-zone and character handoff
    - **1.4.1** [implemented in metadata] Handoffs have idempotent transfer IDs independent of network session identity, bound to source and destination grants; only one unfinished transfer per entity is allowed. ([architecture](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md); [control-plane code](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/crates/mmorpg-control-plane/src/lib.rs))
    - **1.4.2** [agreed design; pending live transfer] The source keeps live character authority while the destination prepares; the destination accepts the transferable state before source authority is retired. See 3.1.3. ([#145](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/145); [architecture](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md))
    - **1.4.3** [agreed design; pending] Staged destination entities do not tick or accept gameplay commands; acceptance and activation are separate, fenced steps. ([architecture](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md); [#145](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/145))
    - **1.4.4** [agreed design; pending] Source and destination use an explicit cutover tick and character-command sequence; old or duplicate commands fail closed without general replay of arbitrary gameplay actions. ([#145](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/145))
    - **1.4.5** [agreed design; pending] Physical seam corridors remain interaction-light: movement continues, but loot grants, trades, quest turn-ins and other irreversible transactions cannot start there. See 3.2.2. ([#145](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/145))
    - **1.4.6** [agreed design; pending] Ordinary creatures and NPCs turn back before logical-zone seams, preventing cross-zone combat. ([#145](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/145))
    - **1.4.7** [agreed design; pending] Teleportation uses the same fenced transfer protocol as walking across a seam, without requiring a corridor. ([#145](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/145))
    - **1.4.8** [not yet implemented] Accepted/committed control-plane metadata alone does not prove imported, activated and retired live entity state; recovery must reconcile every transfer phase. ([architecture](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md); [#145](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/145))
  - **1.5** Empty zones and suspension
    - **1.5.1** [agreed design; pending] Empty zones remain resident for a host-chosen bounded grace period; adaptive factors start negligible. ([#144](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/144))
    - **1.5.2** [agreed design; pending] Suspension freezes all gameplay by default; no subsystem advances merely because elapsed wall-clock time exists. ([#144](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/144))
    - **1.5.3** [agreed design; pending] Offline progression is opt-in by subsystem, with a bounded deterministic resume transition taking elapsed time as input; authoritative code does not read wall clocks. ([#144](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/144))
    - **1.5.4** [agreed design; pending] Cold checkpoint restore is the correctness path; portal/movement prewarming is only an optimization. See 3.3.1. ([#144](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/144); [#40](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/40))

- **3** Character identity, persistence and economy
  - **3.1** Who owns a character
    - **3.1.1** [agreed design; pending durable implementation] Character identity and record survive network connection/session `PlayerId` changes; a character is not a private world copy. ([#30](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/30); [roadmap](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/ROADMAP.md))
    - **3.1.2** [agreed design; pending] While offline, a global character-service/storage boundary owns durable character facts and routing, without simulating live gameplay. ([#30](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/30); [#145](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/145))
    - **3.1.3** [agreed design; pending] While online, precisely one active zone holds an exclusive durable character lease and is the sole live mutator; logout and cross-zone movement transfer authority. See 1.4.2. ([#30](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/30); [#145](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/145))
    - **3.1.4** [implemented demo boundary] Browser roster IDs and appearance settings are local preview state only; they are not server-authoritative or durable multiplayer characters. ([character creation](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/docs/CHARACTER_CREATION.md); [save/load](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/docs/SAVE_LOAD.md))
  - **3.2** Economically durable actions
    - **3.2.1** [agreed design; pending] An acknowledged inventory, currency, XP, quest or other economically meaningful mutation must survive total zone-host loss via replicated durable storage. ([#40](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/40))
    - **3.2.2** [agreed design; pending] Durable grants carry stable grant/event IDs recorded atomically with the character mutation; repeating a grant cannot duplicate rewards. ([#40](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/40))
    - **3.2.3** [agreed design; pending] Less-durable world state or an ephemeral dungeon may reset, but never replay player rewards already committed under 3.2.2. ([#40](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/40))
    - **3.2.4** [agreed design; pending] A zone checkpoint may include live character continuation, but never becomes a competing durable character authority. See 3.1.3. ([#40](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/40); [#30](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/30))
  - **3.3** Checkpoints, journals and recovery
    - **3.3.1** [agreed design; pending durable storage] Checkpoints are immutable captures at deterministic safe tick boundaries, including lifecycle unload and shutdown. ([#40](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/40))
    - **3.3.2** [agreed design; pending] Checkpoints contain exact zone tick, RNG, persistent world/entity/physics state and content/schema identities needed for deterministic continuation. See 2.2.2. ([#40](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/40); [architecture](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md))
    - **3.3.3** [agreed design; pending] Storage I/O runs asynchronously after capture, outside the zone simulation hot loop, with explicit backpressure. ([#40](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/40); [architecture](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md))
    - **3.3.4** [agreed design; pending] Post-checkpoint journals contain accepted authoritative inputs and external events in tick/sequence order, not a second mirror of every result. ([#40](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/40))
    - **3.3.5** [agreed design; pending] Recovery restores the latest valid checkpoint then deterministically replays its journal; economically durable acknowledgements await the required storage commit. ([#40](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/40))
    - **3.3.6** [agreed design; pending] Logical zones checkpoint independently; whole-world atomic checkpoints are not required. ([#40](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/40))
    - **3.3.7** [agreed design; pending] Demo save/load must restore the same authoritative world/character contract rather than invent a browser-owned per-character world slot. ([#40](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/40); [save/load](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/blob/ee32e27afc0b0c829099c1a62c6208c8f3eac43c/docs/SAVE_LOAD.md))
  - **3.4** Persisted data compatibility
    - **3.4.1** [agreed design; pending] Persisted schemas use explicit versioned migrations; development may accept deliberately chosen resets, but future real player data needs a stronger policy. ([#40](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/40))
    - **3.4.2** [agreed design; pending] Unknown, corrupt, truncated or unsupported durable data fails closed with diagnostics rather than silently repairing or replacing records. ([#40](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/issues/40))

  - **5.5** First shared-world PvP scope
    - **5.5.1** [owner decision; implementation pending] The first shared-world release uses opt-in PvP only: players may mutually agree to duels or enter dedicated arenas. Ordinary shared-world play is safe from unsolicited player attacks; open-world contested-area PvP is excluded from this first release. Both PvP modes must preserve server-authoritative combat and the single-writer rule (see 5.2.1 and 1.1.1). Arena placement, duel location rules and rewards remain undecided. ([owner decision recorded in PR #150](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/pull/150))
    - **5.5.2** [owner decision; implementation pending] Duels take place within the existing authoritative world zone without duplicating the characters or transferring zone ownership. Dedicated arenas run as separate authoritative zone instances; entering and leaving an arena must preserve each character's exclusive gameplay authority through the fenced handoff contract (see 5.5.1, 1.4.2 and 3.1.3). Duel eligibility, arena matchmaking, match lifecycle, death rules and rewards remain undecided. ([owner decision recorded in PR #150](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/pull/150))
    - **5.5.3** [owner decision; implementation pending] In-world duels are nonlethal: when duel damage would reduce a participant to or below 1 HP, that participant instead remains at 1 HP and loses the duel immediately. Duel defeat does not trigger normal death, a corpse, spirit release, or graveyard respawn. How unrelated world hazards, creatures and third-party abilities interact with active duels remains undecided. See 5.5.1, 5.5.2 and 5.2.1. ([owner decision recorded in PR #150](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/pull/150))
    - **5.5.4** [owner decision; implementation pending] During an in-world duel, nonparticipant players cannot damage, heal, buff or debuff either duelist; player-origin effects from spectators must not alter duelist state, including through area effects. Creatures and environmental hazards remain active and are not suppressed or moved to a separate simulation. This keeps duels opt-in and server-authoritative (see 5.5.1, 5.5.2 and 5.2.1); the nonlethal 1 HP protection in 5.5.3 applies to duel damage, not general PvE. How an external PvE death or interruption determines the duel result remains undecided. ([owner decision recorded in PR #150](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/pull/150))
    - **5.5.5** [owner decision; implementation pending] If a participant dies from creature or environmental damage during an active in-world duel, normal authoritative PvE death and respawn rules apply, and the other surviving duelist wins even though the opponent did not deal the killing blow. This does not change the nonlethal 1 HP rule for duel-caused damage (see 5.5.3) or the exclusion of outside-player effects (see 5.5.4). Simultaneous PvE deaths and their deterministic winner/draw resolution remain undecided. ([owner decision recorded in PR #150](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/pull/150))
