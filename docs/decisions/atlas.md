- **How to use this atlas**
  - This Atlas is the durable place for **what the product should be**. It has exactly **two semantic leaf-entry kinds: Decision and Question**. Navigation branches organize entries and are not a third kind. Existing leaves not explicitly marked `[question]` are Decisions.
  - A **Decision** is owner-selected product intent and remains in force until a later Decision supersedes it. A **Question** is a consequential unresolved choice. New Question leaves are marked `[question]`; an unanswered Question is not automatically a blocker.
  - Coding agents retrieve the relevant Atlas entries before making consequential product choices. Follow applicable Decisions. If an unanswered Question materially affects the current task, ask the owner **one question at a time**. If work exposes a new consequential fork, append a Question instead of choosing silently.
  - When a Question is answered, preserve it and append a Decision that says `Answers X.Y`. When intent changes, preserve the old Decision and append a Decision that says `Supersedes X.Y`. Do not introduce parallel semantic categories such as constraints, deferred decisions, assumptions, or principles; express those meanings in Decision/Question text and relationships.
  - Each bold dotted number identifies exactly one entry or navigation branch; for example, `1.3.2` is the runtime-partition Decision. References such as `see 1.3.3` refer to these IDs, not line numbers or automatically numbered Markdown lists.
  - This is historical and append-only: add the next unused child number at the end of its parent's children. Never renumber, reorder, remove or reuse an existing ID. A new child may be inserted physically under its parent; concurrent edits to one parent must reconcile ID allocation before merging.
  - Bracketed implementation status on Decisions describes evidence **on 2026-10-11**, not another Atlas entry kind or live completion tracking. `implemented` means demonstrated in current code/docs; `implemented in reference` is explicitly not a production distributed deployment; `agreed design; pending` comes from existing open design issues.
  - Source links point to the inspected repository commit or issue. Existing ADRs/specifications remain authoritative for detailed contracts; issues own work, code/tests own implementation evidence, and this Atlas owns durable product intent and unresolved product Questions.

Each match selects one format and has two equally sized opposing teams; authoritative player and match state remain owned by its single arena zone instance (see 5.5.2 and 1.1.1). Matchmaking, victory conditions, arena defeat/recovery, ranking and rewards remain separate decisions. ([owner decision recorded in PR #150](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/pull/150))
    - **5.5.10** [question] What is the first arena match victory condition for the 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3 formats decided in 5.5.9? Choose **A**: one elimination round (last active team wins), **B**: best of three elimination rounds, or **C**: objective-based scoring with respawns. This selects the match lifecycle and winner calculation; arena defeat/death consequences, matchmaking and rewards remain separate decisions. ([owner decision interview in PR #150](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/pull/150))
    - **5.5.11** [owner decision; implementation pending] Answers 5.5.10: each 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3 arena match consists of **one elimination round**, with no respawns during the round. A team wins when its opponent has no active combatants remaining while it retains at least one; there are no additional rounds or objective-score requirements. How eliminated players are represented and how simultaneous elimination is adjudicated remain separate decisions. See 5.5.9 and 5.5.2. ([owner decision recorded in PR #150](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/pull/150))
    - **5.5.12** [question] What happens to a combatant eliminated during a single-round arena match (see 5.5.11)? Choose **A**: nonlethal knockout at 1 HP, becoming a noninteractive spectator until the match ends; **B**: normal lethal character death with no in-round respawn and post-match recovery; or **C**: an arena-specific downed state that surviving teammates can revive within the same round, but no respawns. Death penalties, rewards and simultaneous team elimination remain separate questions. ([owner decision interview in PR #150](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/pull/150))
    - **5.5.13** [owner decision; implementation pending] Answers 5.5.12: a combatant defeated in a dedicated arena enters an **arena-specific downed state** that an active teammate may revive before the current round ends. Downed players cannot continue normal combat while downed, and there are **no arena respawns** during the round (see 5.5.11). Revival timing, ability/cost and full team-elimination edge cases remain undecided until arena implementation. ([owner decision recorded in PR #150](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/mmorpg/pull/150))
    - **5.5.14** [owner decision; implementation pending] Further detailed arena mechanics are deferred until a playable arena slice is being planned. The arena intent in 5.5.1 through 5.5.13 remains recorded, but the current product-design priority is **foundational shared-world and PvE gameplay**, not resolving more arena edge cases or beginning arena implementation.
